# Project Lilith SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Project Lilith is a personal AI assistant operated solely for its owner. SMS messages are exchanged only with the owner’s opted-in phone number.

## Information Collected

Project Lilith processes the owner’s phone number and the messages exchanged with the service so it can provide conversational replies and requested notifications.

## How Information Is Used

This information is used only to operate Project Lilith’s conversational SMS features.

## Information Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent records, and messaging information are not sold, rented, shared, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

## Message Details

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.

## Contact

For assistance, reply HELP to the Project Lilith phone number.
