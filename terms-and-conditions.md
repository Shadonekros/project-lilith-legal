# Project Lilith SMS Terms and Conditions

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Project Lilith provides conversational AI replies and user-requested notifications to its sole opted-in owner.

## Consent

The owner opts in by texting START to the Project Lilith phone number. Consent is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

## Messages

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. After opting out, no further messages will be sent unless the owner texts START again.

## Privacy

Mobile information is not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
